Who pays for NEAR's security?

Every epoch NEAR mints new tokens to pay validators. This page measures, from block headers, how much is minted, how much users burn in gas, and how much NEAR Intents revenue buys back. The long-term case for NEAR is that the last number catches the first.

The ledger

Minted and burnt NEAR are read from total_supply in block headers at the first and last block of every epoch. USD figures use the NearBlocks daily price. Intents volume and revenue come from DefiLlama and are third-party data.

Sources and limits

Limits: the buyback is taken from DefiLlama's reading of NEAR's revenue accounts. It is checked against the on-chain NEAR balance of those accounts (claim C15) but not re-derived trade by trade. Bought NEAR is held, not burnt, so it lowers float, not supply. Price, transactions and active accounts come from an indexer. Nothing on this page is investment advice.
